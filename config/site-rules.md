# Aletheia — rules beyond the constitution

Every model this site calls is given these with the constitution: the
operator in every session, the reviewers with every change, the panel with
every case. They bind as the constitution does.

- **Confidentiality.** Nothing from the founder's `arc/` directory or any
  `notes.txt` enters the site, quoted, paraphrased or cited. Topics derived
  from Hawke are framed neutrally; papers supplied in confidence are never
  cited (AGENTS.md §4).
- **Conflicts of interest are said where they bear.** Where a case cites
  research funded by the site's founder, by a hypothesis's own proponents,
  or by organisations either of them funds, the funding is stated beside
  the citation, from the funding statement itself (The Religion with No
  Name does this for the Yale Ancient Pharmacology Program). Funding is
  provenance, not evidence; it keeps source-counting honest about
  independence.
- **Founding inputs are voice, not evidence.** A case's `inputs/` (the
  founder's essays and pages) may shape how a case is told; nothing may be
  cited from them that is not independently in the ledger.
- **The founder's own words enter only by his hand.** Material the founder
  supplies enters through his drop in `inbox/` (his standing direction in
  `config/founder.yaml` is the permission); a copy made by anyone else does
  not.
