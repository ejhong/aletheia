## Cases

### Case 1 — Vasocomputation and Embodied Tension

**Slug:** `vasocomputation`

**Subtitle:** Could persistent neurovascular states help explain palpable “knots,” rapid breath-linked release, and embodied memory?

**Overall demo assessment:** Mixed

**Assessment summary:** Several lower-level observations are plausible or measurable, while the proposed vascular latch mechanism and broader computational interpretation remain unresolved.

**Central crux:** Does slow exhalation cause a localized, objective change at a tender point beyond pressure, elapsed time, expectation, and generalized relaxation?

**Best conventional alternative:** Tender points and rapid relief arise from neuromuscular guarding, pain modulation, attention, expectation, fascial mechanics, and generalized autonomic regulation without requiring vascular memory or computation.

**Root claims:**

- `VASO-C001` — Discrete tender or resistant points can be reproducibly localized in the body.
- `VASO-C002` — At least some localized points differ objectively from nearby control tissue.
- `VASO-C003` — Slow exhalation contributes causally to rapid local release beyond pressure and expectation.
- `VASO-C004` — Vascular smooth-muscle latch behavior materially contributes to the persistent local state.
- `VASO-C005` — Long-lived neurovascular states can encode prior defensive or stress responses.
- `VASO-C006` — Vascular networks perform computation relevant to cognition or behavior.

Suggested hierarchy:

- C006 depends on C005.
- C005 depends partly on C004.
- C004 is one possible mechanism for C003 and C002.
- C001 is an observational foundation but does not by itself establish any higher claim.

Suggested demo assessments:

- C001: Provisionally supported
- C002: Mixed
- C003: Unresolved
- C004: Weakly supported
- C005: Unresolved
- C006: Presently untestable

Suggested research opportunity:

**Pressure-by-breath factorial study with blinded local measurements**

Compare sustained pressure plus slow exhalation, sustained pressure plus ordinary breathing, guided slow breathing without local pressure, and sham attention. Measure local stiffness, perfusion, electrical activity, pain, and range of motion.

## Demo evidence for Vasocomputation

Create a balanced set of clearly illustrative Evidence records:

- An observation record supporting reproducible localization, with limitations about examiner agreement and subjective reporting.
- A measurement record supporting localized physical differences, with limitations about small samples and inconsistent methods.
- A contextual physiology record showing that slow breathing changes generalized autonomic state, while explicitly noting that this does not establish localized release.
- An undermining record noting the absence of a decisive pressure-by-breath factorial test.
- A mechanism-qualifying record noting that smooth-muscle latch physiology can exist without proving that a palpable tender point is a latched microvessel.
- An undermining record noting that broader “vascular computation” conclusions require evidence beyond local tissue changes.
