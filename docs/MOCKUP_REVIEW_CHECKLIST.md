# Mockup Review Checklist

Do not begin Phase 2 until the product owner has reviewed these questions.

## Identity and first impression

- [ ] Does the site feel intellectually serious rather than ideological?
- [ ] Is the mission understandable within ten seconds?
- [ ] Does the visual design feel distinctive enough to become an Athanatos property?
- [ ] Is the working name right, or should it change?

## Overview experience

- [ ] Can a general reader understand a Case without opening the graph?
- [ ] Is the current assessment prominent but not overbearing?
- [ ] Are the central crux and competing explanation easy to find?
- [ ] Does the overview feel like an excellent long-form article rather than a database dump?

## Descent into detail

- [ ] Is it obvious which sentences or sections connect to Claims?
- [ ] Can a reader move from a broad thesis to a fine-grained Claim without becoming lost?
- [ ] Are parent, child, and dependency relationships understandable?
- [ ] Is the Claim statement clearly separated from commentary?

## Evidence

- [ ] Are supporting and undermining evidence structurally equal?
- [ ] Is the difference between Evidence and Source understandable?
- [ ] Are source statement and editor inference visibly distinct?
- [ ] Is credibility clearly different from diagnosticity?
- [ ] Are limitations and negative evidence easy to find?

## Assessment

- [ ] Do the assessment labels feel fair and useful?
- [ ] Does the design avoid a simplistic red-versus-green verdict?
- [ ] Is uncertainty visible without becoming evasive?
- [ ] Is “what would change our mind” prominent enough?

## Research agenda

- [ ] Does the Case naturally lead to a decisive experiment or analysis?
- [ ] Can a funder understand why one project matters more than another?
- [ ] Are information gain, cost, feasibility, and affected Claims presented clearly?

## Trust and provenance

- [ ] Is the last review date visible?
- [ ] Is AI involvement disclosed without clutter?
- [ ] Does the change history increase trust?
- [ ] Are demonstration materials unmistakably labeled?

## Usability

- [ ] Does the experience work on a phone?
- [ ] Are long pages navigable?
- [ ] Are filters comprehensible?
- [ ] Is search useful?
- [ ] Are typography and contrast comfortable for long reading?

## Decision

- [ ] Approve the mockup for Phase 2.
- [ ] Approve with specified revisions.
- [ ] Revise the information architecture before further engineering.
