The founder sent the science.org landing page
(science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aec8045); only the DOI is listed above, because

What the DOI resolves to, from Crossref metadata retrieved 2026-09-04 — recorded
as discovery context, not as a verified source record:

- **Title:** Observation of the quantum phase of free fall and the consistency
  with the equivalence principle
- **Journal:** Science Advances, journal article, published 2026-09-04
- **Authors:** Or Dobkowski; Barak Trok; Peter Skakunenko; Yonathan Japha; David
  Groswasser; Maxim Efremov; Chiara Marletto; Ivette Fuentes Guridi; **Roger
  Penrose**; Vlatko Vedral; Wolfgang P. Schleich; Ron Folman
- **Reported:** a cold-atom interferometer in which one wave packet is held
  static in the laboratory frame while the other falls freely; the measured
  relative phase is said to confirm the predicted gauge phase, indicating that
  the equivalence principle may be applied to the quantum domain at this energy
  scale. The abstract adds that the interferometer "opens the door for further
  probing of the latter interface."

Three things whoever processes this should carry forward rather than rediscover:

1. **It does not test objective reduction, and must not be filed as though it
   did.** This measures the quantum phase of free fall against the equivalence
   principle. It says nothing about gravitationally induced collapse, about a
   collapse lifetime scaling as ℏ/E_G, or about microtubules, anaesthesia or
   consciousness. A Penrose-co-authored quantum-gravity experiment is very easy
   to mistake for Orch OR gaining experimental support; it is not that. If it
   enters the ledger, the honest direction is `context`, not `supports`.
2. **Its real bearing on this case is as a possible platform, not as evidence.**
   ORCH-R007 asks for a direct test of the ℏ/E_G collapse scaling and names
   levitated nanoparticles, nanomechanical resonators, and the Howl-Penrose-
   Fuentes condensate scheme as candidate routes. An interferometer that holds
   one wave packet static against another in free fall, built by the group that
   just demonstrated it, is a fourth candidate route worth assessing against
   that research item. That is a research-agenda question (does R007 gain a
   feasible platform?), not a claim-grading one.
3. **Penrose is an author, so it is not independent of the programme** for
   anything it might be read to say about his collapse proposal (AGENTS.md
   3.10). For the equivalence-principle result itself the other eleven authors
   are an experimental collaboration rather than the theory's proponents, so the
   measurement stands on its own; the non-independence bites only if the record
   is later used to support ORCH-C030 or ORCH-C031.
