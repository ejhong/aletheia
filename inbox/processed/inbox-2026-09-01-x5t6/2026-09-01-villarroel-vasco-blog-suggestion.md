Beatriz suggests the transients case look at this VASCO network post
(2026-07-11), which summarizes the spring's replication and validation
work — Doherty 2026a/b, Sinkkoinen, Cann 2026a/b/c, Hayes, Busko 2026a/b
(the Hamburg-plate and coma-aberration studies, including a triple
transient with stellar coma) — plus methodological recommendations for
replication attempts (which sample to start from, why the ~5,399-object
Solano sample is inappropriate for shadow statistics) and a stated plan
to release the full ML-scored catalogue with per-event timestamps and
probabilities once the machine-learning paper clears revision.

A proponent-side summary: discovery pointers, not evidence. Every paper
it names must be verified against its primary record before anything
enters the ledger, and several are already carried (the 2026-08-24
intake covered much of this reading list).
