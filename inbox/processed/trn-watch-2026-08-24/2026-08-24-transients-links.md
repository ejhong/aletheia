# Two items from the literature watch, 2026-08-24

Surfaced by `proposals/watch/watch-2026-08-24-7806/transients.yaml`. The
other three items in that run (Doherty 2605.01190, Cann 2604.16470, Bruehl
et al. 2604.18799) were checked and are already in the ledger as
SRC-DOHERTY-2026B, SRC-CANN-2026B and SRC-BRUEHL-2026-ML — the last one
under its Scientific Reports DOI rather than its arXiv id, which is why the
watch re-surfaced it.

Both items below propose an explanation for the transients. Neither bears
on the crux (TRN-C020, whether any mark was made by light entering the
telescope); both presuppose it. Record accordingly.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06234
  Cann, "Storm-Driven Suppression and Post-Storm Enhancement of Photographic
  Plate Transient Detections at Geosynchronous Altitude". Third Cann
  preprint in the case. Proposes a *natural* near-Earth mechanism —
  magnetospherically trapped icy dust aggregates — which is a third family
  of explanation the case does not currently carry: real sky events that
  are neither plate defects nor artificial reflectors. Note the paper opens
  by treating its own predecessor as having "ruled out emulsion defects",
  which the ledger does not accept.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10061
  Zhitnitsky, "Mysterious Transients in the Palomar Observatory Sky Survey
  (POSS-1) as profound manifestation of the Dark Matter physics". Axion
  quark nugget model applied to the transients. First engagement from
  outside the astronomy/plate-forensics community.
