Deep Memory begins with proposed echoes of Göbekli Tepe in other cultures. Bird figures, handled shapes, animal carvings, culture-bringer stories, and flood traditions are the starting leads. The question is whether any of these comparisons can support a history of shared inheritance.

## The first comparisons

Begin with the objects: establish what each image depicts, where it comes from, and how it is dated. Then ask whether the resemblance is distinctive, and what could explain it. The stories will have their own source histories and tests.

A resemblance, a shared tradition, and a lost civilization are different propositions. Each needs evidence of its own. Göbekli Tepe is the starting point for the investigation; its role in any proposed transmission remains a question.

This is an opening question, not an assessment. No claims, sources, or evidence have been recorded in this case yet.
