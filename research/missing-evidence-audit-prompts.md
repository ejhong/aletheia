# Missing-evidence audit prompts

Eight paste-ready deep-research prompts. Source lists taken from `origin/main` on 2026-08-23 (the live ledger, including Meissner & Penrose 2025 on CCC).

**How to run.** Paste one prompt per case into ChatGPT Deep Research (primary sweep), then Claude Research (judgment / “was this answered?”), then Gemini Deep Research if you want Google-index breadth. For CCC, Orch OR, and transients, also run Kimi Researcher with the same prompt plus: “Prioritize Chinese-language and other non-English primary literature the English tools miss.”

**What to do with the output.** Drop the report in `inbox/<case>/` or paste it in chat. Every citation gets independent verification before it enters the ledger. Deep-research tools invent and garble references; do not treat a report as a source list.

**Shared output format** (already in each prompt): for every item, give authors, year, exact title, venue, DOI and/or arXiv id, URL if no identifier, one-sentence source statement (what the paper itself claims), why it is not already on the list, and whether it is the same team, a dependent reanalysis, or an independent dataset/lab.
