## What I found

**The Greenland platinum's timing.** The case calls the GISP2 platinum anomaly "the strongest thread", and in its closing section it is the residue that keeps the hypothesis alive. The case said only that the spike lies "near the onset". Green, Baldini, Brown et al. (PLOS ONE, September 2025, read in full from PMC) put the GISP2 platinum series on the GICC05 timescale, the one used for the cooling itself. They checked it against annual NGRIP oxygen-isotope layers, volcanic sulphate in both hemispheres' ice, and a Spanish stalagmite. They find the spike about 45 years after the cooling's onset. They say this confirms three earlier analyses (Svensson et al. 2020; Abbott et al. 2021; Holliday et al. 2023), and that their own work was "conducted independently and in parallel with these works, without any knowledge of their results." I read Svensson et al. 2020 in full; its section 3.1 states "The Pt spike occurs about 45 years after the volcanic quadruplet". The limit is recorded on every record: all the analyses re-date one measurement series from one core.

**A volcanic forcing at the onset.** Abbott et al. 2021 (QSR; I read the published open-access version from the Aarhus repository) reconstruct a "~110-year cluster of four major bipolar volcanic signals" just before the onset. Its Northern Hemisphere forcing exceeds the most volcanic periods of the Common Era. It is recorded as a correctly timed forcing, not a demonstrated cause. Comparable eruptions recur in the glacial record (Green et al. rank the onset eruptions 16th and 17th of 1,113).

**The volcanic-source argument (weaker).** Green et al. also argue that the spike's element ratios sit nearest volcanic gas condensates, and that its 14-year spread fits an Icelandic fissure eruption. Their measurements show that Laacher See tephra is platinum-poor. I carried this as *weak* evidence against the impact reading of the proxies. The authors concede they "cannot rule out" an unusual meteorite and that diffusion in the ice could shape the spike's profile; both concessions are quoted.
