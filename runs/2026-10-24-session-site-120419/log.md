# Elusive by Law — first operator pass

## Why this case

The founder's inbox was empty, and no change of mine was held. `mpi` was the only case marked "last worked never": seeded on 2026-08-22, its one edition a mechanical migration 46 days old, its literature never searched since seeding. The charter puts the least-worked case first among the weak ones, so this session went there.

## What I looked for

- **New correlation-matrix (CMM) results since the 2023 online preprint.** Searches of the web, the JSE and the Walach, Grote, Flores and Maier lines found no new published matrix replication. The Flores & Rapazote-Flores preprint has a second version (Qeios, 2024-01-26). Its abstract reports the same split as v1: Random.org significant (p = 0.0001), Mersenne Twister not (p = 0.069), at 94% vs 67% power. It is still a preprint. Recorded as a duplicate in `dispositions.yaml`.
- **Anything that would show the current account wrong.** This meant chiefly a test of MPI's decline "fine structure", which the article said had never been tested as a prediction, and a statement from the proponents of what would refute the model. The article's agenda said nobody had asked for one.

## What I found and admitted

Four sources, each read through `scripts/site.ts read`. Eight evidence records (MPI-E018–E025), two claims (MPI-C504, MPI-C505) and one research item (MPI-R006).

1. **Dechamps & Maier 2019 (JSE 33(3), open-access LMU copy).** A Munich group extended MPI's decline idea into a damped-oscillation shape. They fitted it to two smoker studies, preregistered a forecast for 203 new participants, and scored it against 10,000 simulated runs from the same generator. Simulated runs fit the forecast better 18.71% of the time on area, 19.42% on turning point and 5.28% on end point. That is not significant on any preregistered statistic (E018 → new featured claim C504). Their post hoc measures did separate the data at 1%, by their account (E019, *qualifies*, kept for fairness). The authors state that their systematic pattern is "one critical difference from the MPI". So this tests a cousin of the model, not its core, and I have weighted it that way.
2. **Maier, Dechamps & Pflitsch 2018 (Frontiers in Psychology).** 12,571 participants, BF01 = 10.07 for the null. The authors related a post-hoc oscillation, compared against one simulated control run, to MPI (E020, *undermines* C505). The same paper quotes Maier & Dechamps: under the model, "real null effects … cannot be distinguished from decline effects" (E022 → C010). It also records their earlier smoker effect, BF10 = 66.7, followed by a preregistered replication at BF01 = 11.07 (E025 → C502).
3. **Grote 2018 (Frontiers commentary).** Grote ran 1,000 permutations of those data. Chance reproduces the oscillation at p = 0.328, and a running total's swings shrink as data accumulate whatever is there (E021 → catalog claim C505).
4. **von Lucadou 2005, with Guiard's reply (Zeitschrift für Anomalistik 5: 93–125, German).** Lucadou: the MPI "nicht unfalsifizierbar"; reliable signal transfer "würde es sofort zu Fall bringen". He then predicts that every psi experiment with reliable transfer contains a sensory leak (E023, *qualifies* C010). Guiard's rejoinder asks for "ein präzises Kriterium" separating a PK process from chance. He argues that forbidding the correlation density as a criterion rejects the only one there is (E024, *supports* C010). German is quoted verbatim, with translations in the inference, marked as the editor's.

## Judgment

- **The case verdict is unchanged** (weakly_supported). The new tests bear on whether decline can be made testable and on the falsifiability crux. They do not bear on the attribution contest, because the tests that failed target an extension the authors distinguish from MPI.
- **MPI-C010 (insulated from disconfirmation in practice): mixed → provisionally_supported.** The model's author does name a refuting outcome, but he assigns it in advance to a leak, and it lies outside the matrix experiment the model nominated. The critic's criterion went unsupplied in that exchange. Sympathetic researchers concede the indistinguishability. Held short of well_supported because the 2021 team conceded the paradigm is not replicable, and Lucadou's 2007/2015 texts were not checked for a sharper criterion. If they supply one, the claim returns to mixed; the assessment says so.
- **MPI-C504 is featured and graded well_supported.** It is a documentary statistical claim about one paper.
- I also corrected an inherited error in the assessment. MPI-C002's and MPI-C008's objections called Walach 2020 "preregistered" or "preregistration-era". The 2026-08-25 audit had removed that wording from the article but left it in the migrated assessment. Both now describe the study as the record does: large, two-lab, evaluated against ten thousand simulations.
- **The article** (1,824 → 2,228 words) corrects "never been tested as predictions". The model's own corollaries remain untested; a cousin was tested once and failed. The crux section now reports the 2005 exchange. The agenda adds the frozen test of the post hoc measures (MPI-R006), and it narrows the request to the proponents: name a refutation *within* their own experiment, since the one given in 2005 lay outside it. It also adds that the online preprint's power was unequal between its two sources.
